### 🏛️ Mathematical Foundations & Systems Architecture

```math
\mathrm{d}S_t = \mu S_t \mathrm{d}t + \sigma S_t \mathrm{d}W_t \qquad \mathbf{\Sigma} = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{\Lambda} \mathbf{V}^T \qquad \text{VWAP} = \frac{\sum P_i V_i}{\sum V_i}
```

My work combines formal training in **applied mathematics** with **high-performance systems engineering**:

- 📐 **Mathematics & Quantitative Modeling:** Stochastic calculus, continuous-time dynamics, time-series econometrics, spectral analysis, dispersion matrices, and market microstructure.
- ⚙️ **Low-Latency & Causal Systems:** Building deterministic backtesters with prefix-stability validation, strict intra-bar execution causality, and zero-lookahead guarantees.
- 🎨 **Visual Systems & Computational Graphics:** Interactive financial terminals, 3D surface/volatility visualizations with **Three.js**, and high-throughput web frontends utilizing **WebAssembly** for client-side compute.
